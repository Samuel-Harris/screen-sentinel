Pieces for Developers is a code snippet manager and AI copilot that lives inside your IDE. Screen Sentinel captures your entire screen and audio 24/7 — every app, every window, every conversation. they solve different problems and many developers use both.

## why Screen Sentinel?

### captures everything, not just code

Pieces only sees what's inside your editor. Screen Sentinel captures your browser research, Slack conversations, Zoom calls, terminal output, design tools — everything on your screen. when your AI asks "what was I working on?", Screen Sentinel has the full picture.

### audio + meetings

Screen Sentinel transcribes all audio — meetings, calls, pair programming sessions. Pieces has no audio capabilities. when someone mentions a deadline in a Zoom call, Screen Sentinel captures it.

### pipes — automated workflows

Screen Sentinel's pipe ecosystem (16+ published pipes, 574+ installs on the most popular) automates workflows: time tracking, CRM sync, daily summaries, meeting intelligence. Pieces doesn't have scheduled automations on your screen data.

### 45+ app connections

Screen Sentinel connects to Slack, Notion, Gmail, Obsidian, Toggl, HubSpot, Salesforce, and 40+ more apps. Pieces connects primarily to IDEs.

## when to use Pieces instead

Pieces excels at **code snippet management** — it parses code, extracts metadata (language, repo context, related docs), and organizes snippets with tags. if your primary need is saving and finding code snippets inside your editor, Pieces is purpose-built for that.

## use both together

many developers run both:
- **Pieces** for snippet management inside the editor
- **Screen Sentinel** for everything else — meetings, browser research, Slack, the full visual timeline of your day

they complement each other. Screen Sentinel gives your AI the full context of your workday; Pieces gives deep code-specific context in your IDE.

## frequently asked questions

### is Screen Sentinel better than Pieces for Developers?

they solve different problems. Screen Sentinel captures your entire screen and audio 24/7 — everything you see, say, or hear. Pieces manages code snippets inside your IDE. Screen Sentinel is better for full-context AI memory, meeting transcription, and automated workflows. Pieces is better for code snippet organization. many developers use both.

### can Screen Sentinel replace Pieces?

for code snippet management inside your editor, Pieces is more specialized. but for everything Pieces can't do — screen capture, audio transcription, meeting notes, time tracking, CRM sync, browser research recall — Screen Sentinel fills the gap. if you want one tool for everything, Screen Sentinel covers more ground.

### does Screen Sentinel work with Cursor and Claude Code like Pieces does?

yes. Screen Sentinel has an MCP server that works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, Continue, Gemini CLI, and more. your AI coding assistant can search your full screen history, not just code snippets.
